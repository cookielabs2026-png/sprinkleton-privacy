Privacy Policy for Sprinkleton: Candy Match 3

Effective date: October 13, 2026

Sprinkleton: Candy Match 3 ("Sprinkleton", "the app") is a free match-3 puzzle game published by Cookie Labs, an individual developer ("we", "us"). We are responsible for the personal data described in this policy. You can contact us at any time at cookielabs2026@gmail.com.

In short: Sprinkleton has no accounts and we run no servers. Your progress stays on your phone. The game shows ads from Google AdMob only when you choose to watch one. Usage statistics and crash reports are sent only if you say yes, and you can change that at any time in Settings → Privacy & Ads. We do not sell your data for money.

Your choices

The first time you open Sprinkleton, before any ads, analytics or crash reporting start, the game asks you to choose:

  • Accept — usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase) and crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) are switched on, and ads may be personalized, subject to any consent choices described below.
  • No, thanks — analytics and crash reporting stay off, and the ads you choose to watch are not personalized. The game plays exactly the same.

You can change your mind at any time in Settings → Privacy & Ads, which has:

  • Share usage & crash data (on/off). Turning it off stops collection immediately and deletes crash reports and analytics data still waiting on your device;
  • Personalized ads (on/off). When off, every ad request asks Google for non-personalized ads;
  • Ad privacy choices, shown where the law requires it (the UK, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws). It opens Google's consent form, where you can give, refuse or withdraw consent, or opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your data for ads;
  • View privacy policy.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress — level, score, stars, coins, hearts, power-ups, daily-bonus streak and settings, including your privacy choices — is stored only on your own device, with a small on-device backup copy so progress survives the system clearing app storage. It may also be included in your device's standard Android backup, which Google manages under your device settings. It is not uploaded to us, and it is removed if you uninstall the app or clear its data. Sprinkleton never asks for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Sprinkleton shows rewarded video ads from Google AdMob only when you choose to watch one in exchange for in-game rewards. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's ads SDK collects your device's advertising ID and app set ID, your IP address (from which an approximate location is estimated), information about your interactions with ads and the app, and diagnostic information about how the app and SDK perform. Where available, it may also use Android's Privacy Sandbox features (such as Topics and attribution reporting).

If you choose "No, thanks", turn off Personalized ads, or refuse consent in Google's form, ads are requested as non-personalized: they are based on things like the app and your general location rather than your interests, although Google still uses the information above to deliver them, limit how often you see them, and detect fraud. Ad content is limited to Google's "General audiences" rating.

Google is an independent controller of the data it collects for advertising. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's advertising policies. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Analytics and crash reports (only if you agree)

If you choose Accept, or later turn on Share usage & crash data, Sprinkleton uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics to understand how the game is played (for example, which levels are too hard) and to find and fix crashes. They collect:

  • gameplay events, for example a level started, won or lost, a booster used, a reward ad watched, a setting changed, or an in-app purchase made or failed (the product — never payment details);
  • a per-install app-instance ID and Firebase installation ID (not your advertising ID — analytics never collects it);
  • device model, Android version, app version, language, and an approximate (country/city) location from your IP address;
  • if the app crashes or a purchase fails with an error: technical crash logs and the state of the app at the time.

Analytics is never linked to advertising: we keep Google's ad-related analytics settings switched off. If you say no, or turn sharing off later, the game sends none of this. Google processes this data on our behalf as our service provider. Analytics data is kept for no more than 14 months, and crash reports for 90 days.

Reminders (notifications)

Sprinkleton can show optional local reminders (for example, when your hearts are refilled or a daily reward is ready). They are scheduled entirely on your device, send no data anywhere, need your permission, and can be turned off in Settings or in your device's notification settings.

Game settings file and updates

When it starts, the app may download a small game-balance settings file from our page on GitHub Pages. No personal data is sent with this request beyond what any web request includes, such as your IP address. We do not receive or log it; GitHub, which hosts the file, handles that request data under its own privacy statement. The app also asks Google Play whether a newer version is available, so it can offer you the update.

In-app purchases

Sprinkleton offers optional coin packs and a starter pack. Purchases are processed by Google Play Billing; we never receive or store your payment details.

Deleting your data

Turning off Share usage & crash data stops collection and clears the analytics data and crash reports still on your device. Settings → Reset progress erases your progress and settings, switches analytics off, clears its data, and asks the privacy question again. Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on your device. Analytics and crash data already sent to Google is deleted automatically at the end of the retention periods above. We hold no name, email or account for you, so we cannot look up data by person; for anything else, email cookielabs2026@gmail.com.

Why we use your data (legal bases)

For players in the UK and Switzerland, we rely on:

  • your consent for analytics, crash reporting and personalized ads — you can withdraw it at any time in Settings → Privacy & Ads;
  • our legitimate interests in funding a free game with non-personalized ads you choose to watch, and in preventing ad fraud;
  • performing our agreement with you when you make a purchase.

Who receives data

Only Google (AdMob, Firebase, Play Billing and Play app updates) and, for ads, the advertising partners Google works with under its own policies. We do not sell personal data for money, and we do not share it with anyone else, except if the law requires us to.

International transfers

Google processes data in the United States and other countries. Google relies on the UK Extension to the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, and Standard Contractual Clauses to protect data transferred from the UK and Switzerland.

Security

All data the app sends is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).

Your rights

UK and Switzerland

You have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing. You can also complain to your local data-protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office; in Switzerland, the FDPIC).

United States (California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut and other states with privacy laws)

In the last 12 months, Sprinkleton has collected these categories of personal information: identifiers (advertising ID, app-instance and installation IDs), internet or other electronic activity (game and ad interactions), approximate geolocation, and commercial information (in-app purchase history, if you allowed analytics). We do not collect sensitive personal information.

We do not sell personal information for money. However, letting Google AdMob use your advertising ID for personalized ads may count as "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising, or as "targeted advertising", under these laws. You can opt out at any time by turning off Personalized ads or using Ad privacy choices in Settings → Privacy & Ads. You also have the right to know what we hold about you, to correct or delete it, and to appeal a decision we make about your request. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

How to make a request

Most of these rights can be used directly in the app, through the switches in Settings → Privacy & Ads and Reset progress. For anything else, email cookielabs2026@gmail.com. Data held by Google for its own advertising purposes is controlled by Google; you can manage it through your Android settings and Google's tools.

Do Not Track

Sprinkleton is an app, not a website, so it does not receive browser "Do Not Track" signals. Use the controls in Settings → Privacy & Ads instead.

Children

Sprinkleton is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not designed for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe data about a child under 13 has been collected, email us and we will help delete it.

Changes to this policy

If the app or its data practices change, we will update this policy, change the effective date above, and post the new version at this same address before the change takes effect.
